About Course Schedule & Module Planner

Version 3.0 (Multi-Course Edition)

Course Schedule & Module Planner is an interactive web-based curriculum planning tool designed for academic leaders, curriculum designers, and course managers. It streamlines the structure and mapping of multi-year academic courses across 3-Year (12 Terms), 2-Year Direct Entry (8 Terms), and Accelerated Pathway (9 Terms) structures.

✨ Key Capabilities
• Multi-Pathway Structures: 3-Year, 2-Year Direct Entry, & Accelerated Pathway.
• Multi-Course Tabs: Manage multiple course structures simultaneously with tab renaming & pastel themes.
• Interactive Drag & Drop: Drag module widgets across term columns with workload rate calculations.
• Certification Audit Engine: Real-time credit auditing & prerequisite compliance checks.
• JSON Data Export/Import: Save and restore full multi-course workspaces effortlessly.
